Social media account checks are simple, but they eat time.

One account is easy. Dozens of accounts become painful.

Teams need to know whether accounts are logged in, whether content loads, whether messages look normal, and whether scheduled work finished.

## The user problem

The more accounts a team manages, the easier it is to miss something.

Daily checks often include:

- Confirming whether accounts are online.
- Opening apps to see whether feeds load.
- Checking comments and messages.
- Testing media upload.
- Checking publish status.
- Recording abnormal accounts.

None of this is difficult. The problem is doing it every day across many devices.

## A real scene

A short video team may manage accounts across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Xiaohongshu, Weibo, and other apps.

Each platform has different accounts and different tasks.

If one account logs out or one cloud phone gets stuck, the team wants to know today, not tomorrow.

## Turn checking into a task

Account checks can be broken into a repeatable workflow:

- Open the target app.
- Check whether the account reaches the home screen.
- Check whether content loads.
- Record the task result.
- Put abnormal devices into a review list.

This changes the work from "open every phone" to "review the results."

## The difficult part

If a system only shows "failed," the operator still has to open the device and investigate.

A better system should help explain whether the issue is login, popup, network, or script-related.
